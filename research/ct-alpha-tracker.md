## Patterns to Track

### What Early Callers Say
- [ ] Technical language vs hype
- [ ] On-chain analysis vs narrative
- [ ] Specific entry/exit levels
- [ ] Risk warnings included?

### Timing Signals
- [ ] How early before pump?
- [ ] Market cap at call
- [ ] Volume characteristics
- [ ] Social sentiment at time of call

### Red Flags
- [ ] Always bullish (never wrong = suspicious)
- [ ] No skin in the game disclosure
- [ ] Deletes bad calls
- [ ] Paid promotions without disclosure
